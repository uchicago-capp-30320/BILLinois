About BILLinois

The goal of BILLinois is to enable everyone in Illinois to learn about and track legislation they care about.

BILLinois was started in 2025 by a group of six graduate students at the University of Chicago as part of Professor James Turk's course, Software Engineering for Civic Technology. The team includes:

  • Livia Mucciolo
  • Echo Nattinger
  • Joaquin Pinto
  • Caitlin Pratt
  • David Steffen
  • Suchi Tailor
  • Karen Yi
